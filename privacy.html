Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026 · Applies to Ringsure: Ring Sizer for iPhone and iPad

Ringsure collects no data. There is no account, no server of ours, no advertising and no analytics. The only connection it makes is to Apple's App Store, for the optional tips. What you measure stays on your device.

What stays on your device

The sizes you save, with the names, hands, fingers and notes you give them, are stored inside the app on your iPhone or iPad, together with your settings and any screen calibration you make. We never receive any of it. If you delete Ringsure, this data is deleted with it.

Permissions

None. Ringsure does not ask for the camera, photos, location, contacts or any other permission. It reads your device model from iOS only to know the size of your screen's pixels, on the device.

When you share or print

Sharing a size

Share opens the iOS share sheet with a line of text such as your size in each system. It goes only where you choose to send it, under that app's own privacy policy.

The printable ring sizer

The page is made on your device and handed to the iOS print sheet. "Save the sizer as PDF" makes the same page as a temporary PDF file on your device and opens the share sheet, so you can keep it in Files or send it to a print shop. Ringsure does not keep or send it anywhere itself.

Tips

Tips are optional in-app purchases processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. To show their prices and to finish a tip, the app connects to the App Store when it starts. Ringsure never sees your payment details or Apple ID. Apple gives developers only anonymous sales totals. The app may also show Apple's standard rating prompt once; iOS decides whether it appears.

Children

Ringsure collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
